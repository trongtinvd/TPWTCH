EXAMPLE CARDS (each with unresolved rules; see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)

THE VINE - Creature 1/1
"When this card enters and at the beginning of your turn, apply the following effect 4 times. Target the closest
permanent zone to this card that isn't occupied by this card or a Vine token (you choose if ties), destroy all
cards on that zone and create a Vine creature 1/1 on that tile."
  Triggers: PermanentEntered(self), TurnStarted(controller). Repeat 4: find closest legal zone, destroy all cards
  there, create Vine token. Re-evaluate the board each repetition; no legal zone = that repetition does nothing.
  The card defines its own distance function; engine supplies candidate zones + legality.
  UNRESOLVED: creatures are front-row only; may the effect create a Vine in any targeted zone, or only legal
  creature zones? (Q16)

INFINITY WALL - Quick, 6 HP
"Target a column that is fully filled, exile all cards on that column, that column becomes unusable for the rest of
the game. Return this card to your hand."
  Check target column IsFullyOccupied; exile contents; apply permanent column restriction via rule/modifier
  system (not a GameState variable); return card to hand.

FRACTURE GROUND - Slow, 4 HP
"Each zone gains 10 HP (they are unaffected by regular combat). When those zone die, exile the cards on that zone,
it becomes unusable."
  ZoneIntegrity(+10); integrity immune to regular combat damage; at <= 0 exile contents + mark unusable.
  UNRESOLVED: "each zone" = permanent zones only, or deck/hand/GY/exile/stack too? Stacking of multiple
  integrity effects (Q10).

MAXIMUM DAMAGE - Slow, 0 HP (0 HP still resolves)
"For the next three turns, your distance, speed, and acceleration are treated as 0. When the effect ends, destroy
all cards your opponent controls, you draw 7 cards, and gain life equal to the total HP + total attack of all the
destroyed cards."
  Continuous modifier: effective Distance/Speed/Acceleration = 0 (underlying values unchanged). Duration: 3 turns.
  On expiry: destroy opponent-controlled cards, snapshot their HP/ATK, draw 7, gain life.
  UNRESOLVED: boundary of "next three turns" (Q11).

KING EYE - Creature 6/10
"Your Opponent cannot discern other cards you control."
  Information-system effect; board unchanged; opponent gets restricted observation (presentation may obscure the
  board). Restriction must be reusable by other cards. UNRESOLVED: what "discern" hides (Q5).

AVATAR OF RABIES - Creature 3/7
"Whenever a creature attacks, you may shift this card to an adjacent column. Whenever this card takes combat damage
it deals 1 damage to another creature. Damage dealt by this card has 'This card loses all of its original ability
and gains the abilities of Avatar of Rabies'."
  On CreatureAttackDeclared: optional choice (left/right/stay); movement uses normal zone legality.
  On CombatDamageReceived: 1 damage to another creature (trigger per damage event, not per point, unless a card
  says otherwise). Last clause = ability transformation effect (layered abilities), never deleting the target's
  definition.

THROWING BANDAGES - Quick, 10 HP
"Remove 7 damage counters from any target."
  Player chooses which damage instances to remove. Example: A=7, B=1: may remove B (1) plus six points from A if
  permitted. UNRESOLVED: seven points vs seven total damage (Q9).

THE SEVEN STEVES - Slow, 7 HP
"Exile 7 creatures from your field or GY, each of those creatures deals damage to any non-player target."
  Select 7 creature instances, exile, snapshot, each deals damage. Instances must stay identifiable across zones.
  UNRESOLVED: damage amount per creature unspecified.

PARRIABLE - Slow, 2 HP
"Your opponent gains 'Power double, any damage you deal may miss if you do not press Enter within 2 seconds before
the attack animation lands'. You gain 'Whenever your opponent would deal damage, you may press Enter once to create
0.5 second of parry, if your opponent presses Enter within that window negate the damage'."
  Needs real-time input windows (see ARCHITECTURE.md). Granted abilities are first-class temporary effects.
  UNRESOLVED: ownership/ordering of simultaneous input windows.

======================================================================
DESIGN NOTES NOT COVERED ELSEWHERE
======================================================================

- Stage gimmicks are explicit in the stage description; NPC-exclusive cards/mechanics are allowed.
- Equipment modifies character or card stats/abilities (Current layer only; see RULES.md stat layers).
- Characters have unique 3D models and abilities.
- Card text may leave numbers unspecified; the card definition must state them (e.g. Seven Steves).
- Wrapper-like behavior for rare effects is desired, but implement as registered modifiers (ARCHITECTURE.md).
- Phase 4 goal: add rule-stressing cards to expose gaps, not a large pool.
- Long-term: large card library, complex interactions, NPC-exclusive mechanics, distinct AI personalities,
  hidden-information reasoning, deep look-ahead, tournaments, personality discovery, replays, headless sim,
  regression tests, rich 3D presentation, stage-specific PvE mechanics. Technical goal: a simulation clean and
  generic enough that stranger cards come from composing existing rules. Design goal: meaningful decisions, not
  complexity for its own sake.
